Hi, I'm Vaara! I'm 11 years old and I love making things with my hands.

It started with a ball of wool and a crochet hook. Then came loom bands, painted rocks and bottles, and now a whole collection of little things I've made myself.

Every scrunchie, bracelet and pouch takes time, patience and lots of practice. I'm so happy when someone takes one home. Thank you for supporting a young maker!
